Meet Tachyon, an ultra-fast, modern, and lightweight open-source music player designed for audiophiles and power users who demand pristine audio quality, seamless playback, and an elegant Material 3 interface.

Built with a decoupled, high-performance architecture in Flutter and custom native audio FFI (miniaudio_player), Tachyon delivers zero UI jank, instant O(1) library navigation, and unmatched CPU and RAM efficiency.

🎧 High-Fidelity Audio Engine
- Seamless Continuous Crossfade: Real 25ms crossfade ticker with Equal-Power and Linear curves for perfectly smooth transitions between tracks.
- True Gapless Playback: Enjoy uninterrupted listening with zero delay between consecutive songs.
- Volume Boost & Pitch Control: Boost audio gain up to 200% and fine-tune playback speed (0.5x–1.5x) and pitch in real time.

🎛️ Studio-Grade DSP Audio Effects
- 10-Band Graphic Equalizer: Fine-tune your sound with rich presets and manual controls.
- Bauer Crossfeed (bs2b): Experience speaker-like spatial sound on headphones (Chu Moy & Default presets) to drastically reduce listening fatigue.
- Stereo Widener & Spatializer: Expand or narrow your stereo image (0.0x to 2.0x).
- Freeverb Algorithmic Reverb: Immerse yourself with presets (Small Room, Studio, Concert Hall, Cathedral, Plate, Ambient) or customize room size, damping, width, wet, and dry mix.
- True-Peak Limiter: Prevent digital clipping and distortion at high volumes.

📜 Smart Synchronized Lyrics
- Hierarchical Multi-Source Engine: Resolves lyrics from embedded audio tags (USLT), contiguous local .lrc files, LRCLIB, and lyrics.ovh fallback.
- Real-Time Synced Scrolling: O(log n) active line tracking with smooth auto-scroll, tap-to-seek, and automatic translation support.

⚡ Ultra-Optimized Performance
- Minimized Resource Footprint: Built-in minimized mode suspends rendering, clears GPU image caches, and throttles background timers for maximum battery life and responsiveness.
- Pure Dart Isolate Tag Scanning: Concurrent recursive metadata extraction for FLAC, MP3, M4A, OGG, and OPUS without heavy external CLI binaries.
- 100% In-Memory Relational Graph: Instantaneous browsing and search across artists, albums, tracks, and playlists.

🎨 Modern Material 3 UI
- Adaptive layout for phones, tablets, and desktop.
- Dynamic color themes with OLED true black mode.
- Multi-Language Support: Extensible internationalization (i18n) with dynamic switching and zero hardcoded strings.

🔒 Privacy-Focused & 100% FOSS
- Completely offline-first with zero telemetry, no hidden analytics, and zero ads.
- Free and Open-Source Software licensed under GPL-3.0.
